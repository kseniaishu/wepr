Product two

Seasonal estimate — not a forecast

For a date years out there are no recent observations to condition on, so there is nothing for a forecast model to use. What this gives instead is a climatological expectation: the historical norm for that day of year, adjusted for the record's warming trend. It describes a distribution of plausible days rather than predicting one, and it does not get better or worse the closer the date is.
