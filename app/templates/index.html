Twenty Iberian cities, two different claims

Temperature and precipitation estimates built from the ERA5-Land reanalysis archive. The site serves two products and keeps them apart, because conflating them is the mistake this rebuild exists to fix.

Forecast

Next day

Tomorrow predicted from today's observations, with every predictor knowable before the day it describes. Reported against persistence and day-of-year climatology, because a model that cannot beat both is not worth its complexity.

Not a forecast

Seasonal estimate

For an arbitrary date years out there are no recent observations to condition on, so the answer is a climatological expectation — the historical norm for that day of year, trend-adjusted. It comes as an interval, never a bare number.

Not a projection
